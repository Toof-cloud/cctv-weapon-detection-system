SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY OF FINDINGS (MODELS 1 TO 10)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. The 350-Image Unified External Benchmark Audit (Zero Data Leakage):
   - To satisfy the thesis adviser and panelist mandate for an unbiased evaluation,
     an independent Unified External Benchmark of 350 images was created with
     0% overlap / zero data leakage against every training and validation set of
     Models 1 through 10.
   - Model 10 achieved the highest overall score across all 10 models:
     * mAP@0.50:  62.38% (Model 1: 12.24%, Model 5: 55.05%, Model 8: 59.62%,
                  Model 9: 46.81%, Model 10: 62.38%)
     * Precision: 72.05% (Model 1: 18.91%, Model 8: 55.38%, Model 9: 67.14%,
                  Model 10: 72.05%)
     * Recall:    67.03% (Model 1: 19.05%, Model 9: 51.65%, Model 10: 67.03%)
     * F1-Score:  69.45% (Highest across all 10 models)
     * Hard Negative Rejection Specificity: 84.00% (84/100 clean non-weapon scenes)

2. Root Cause Analysis of Model 9's Knife Recall Truncation:
   - In Model 9, anchor scales were constricted to ((8,), (16,), (32,), (64,), (128,))
     and bounding box regression was tightly clamped to ln(1.4) ≈ 0.3365 to eliminate
     oversized box hallucinations across room fixtures.
   - While this produced record-high precision (94.09%) and only 11 false positives,
     it caused 59 Knife False Negatives on held-out tests because elongated kitchen
     and combat knives exceeding 180 pixels in surveillance views could not be
     proposed by the 128px maximum anchor ceiling.

3. Engineering Solution in Model 10:
   - Restored 5-Stage Anchor Pyramid: Scales ((16,), (32,), (64,), (128,), (256,))
     reinstated full receptive field coverage for long knives without losing small guns.
   - Calibrated 2.2x Clamping: Regression transformed from ln(1.4) to ln(2.2) ≈ 0.7885.
     A 256px anchor expanded up to 2.2x bounds up to 563px blades, completely solving
     knife dropout while maintaining geometric stability against room-wide box explosions.
   - CostiCatargiu NoGUN v5 & WeaponDataset v11 Integration: Injected 450 held
     non-weapon hard negatives (phones, bottles, wallets, umbrellas, selfie sticks,
     tools, mugs, brooms) and 550 weapon scenes (350 knife, 200 pistol), expanding
     the training set to 2,804 images.

4. Empirical Impact in Model 10:
   - On the 279-image Model 9 Held-Out Test Set, Knife AP surged from 50.43% (Model 9)
     to 95.18% (Model 10) (+44.75% increase), overall mAP jumped from 73.34% to 95.22%,
     and recall surged from 73.11% to 95.80% (228 TP / 19 FP / only 10 FN).
   - On the 185-image Unseen Internal CCTV Test Set, Model 10 achieved 97.22% Precision,
     96.55% Recall, 99.90% Knife AP, 95.52% Handgun AP, 97.71% mAP@0.50, and 100%
     Negative Rejection (40/40 clean).
   - On the Held Non-Weapon Grid Audit (18 pure non-weapon scenes), specificity
     improved to 94.44% (17/18 clean) compared to 88.89% in Model 9 and 72.22% in Model 8.
   - On Real Non-Weapon Surveillance Videos (5 clips, 1,517 frames), Model 10 produced
     0 confirmed alerts (100% Specificity), rejecting clothes hangers, drying racks,
     and outdoor railings.

SECTION 3: DATASET PROVENANCE, CITATIONS & SOURCING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. USRT Action Recognition & Firearm Dataset (Primary Handgun CCTV Source):
   - Authors: Ruiz-Santaquiteria, J., Vallez, N., Bueno, G., & Deniz, O.
   - Host / DOI: Mendeley Data, DOI: 10.17632/bbzpxhd22j.2
   - Role: Primary authentic CCTV handgun dataset in Models 5 through 10.
   - Description: Real elevated multi-camera CCTV installations across 140 surveillance
     incident scenes. Solves macro studio scale mismatch (~0.35% frame area).
   - Allocation: 1,140 total project images (902 train, 113 val, 113 test).

2. OD-WeaponDetection: Knife Detection Dataset (Primary Knife Source):
   - Authors / Host: University of Granada (UGR) Computer Vision Group & Dataset Ninja
   - Role: Primary bladed weapon dataset in Models 3 through 10.
   - Description: Multi-angle combat, kitchen, folding, and tactical blades viewed from
     varied distances, ambient lighting, and partial hand-grip occlusions.
   - Allocation: 1,135 total project images (908 train, 113 val, 114 test).

3. DARPA VIRAT Video Dataset Release 2.0 (Public Surveillance Hard Negatives):
   - Sponsoring Agency: Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA) / Kitware Inc.
   - Role: Background negative rejection (Class 0) introduced in Model 6.
   - Description: Real outdoor surveillance footage of parking lots, walkways, and stairs.
   - Allocation: 160 pure non-weapon surveillance frames.

4. USRT No_Gun Walking Pedestrian Subset (Human Empty-Hand Negatives):
   - Authors: Ruiz-Santaquiteria et al. (Mendeley Data)
   - Role: Class 0 training frames teaching the network that pedestrians walking
     with empty hands are not weapon threats.
   - Allocation: 100 images (80 train, 10 val, 10 test).

5. In-House CCTV Commercial Hard Negatives (Model 7, 8, 9 Empirical Mining):
   - Sourced from surveillance incident recordings and real-world commercial CCTV footage:
     * Dining Hall Negatives (60 images): Clip 08 (beverage bottles, cups, trays, wallets).
     * Retail Counter Negatives (48 images): Clips 05/09/10 (POS pinpads, cash drawers).
     * Staged Indoor Rooms (40 images): CAM01/02 Scene 001 (tables, lamps, staircases).
     * Clean Rooms (40 images): Hallways, door moldings, empty offices.
     * Floor Phone Filming (17 images): Horizontal smartphones on floor tiles.
     * Targeted Forensic Crops (296 images): Door hand shadows (CAM2_SCENE002), dark
       ponytail hair (CAM2_SCENE002), counter base molding shadows (CAM2_SCENE001),
       jacket shoulder creases (Unseen 225703), and cashier cap visors/headphones (Unseen 230249).

6. CostiCatargiu YOLOv12 / NoGUN v5 & WeaponDataset v11 (Adviser Enhancement in Model 10):
   - Authors: Constantin Catargiu & Iulian B. Ciocoiu (Gheorghe Asachi Technical University of Iasi)
   - Paper: "Real-Time Weapon Detection Using Enhanced YOLOv12 Models and a Custom Dataset" (2026)
   - Repositories:
     * NoGUN v5: https://universe.roboflow.com/gundetectiondataset/nogun/dataset/5 (CC BY 4.0)
     * WeaponDataset v11: https://universe.roboflow.com/gundetectiondataset/weapondataset-oi2g3/dataset/11
   - Extracted Components:
     * 450 Held Non-Weapon Hard Negatives (Train): Smartphones, water bottles, wallets, keys,
       remotes, umbrellas, selfie sticks, screwdrivers, pliers, floor brooms, coffee mugs.
     * 350 Knife Scenes (Train): Tactical, kitchen, multi-angle, and edge-on blades.
     * 200 Pistol Scenes (Train): Handgun draws, magazine reloads, two-handed grip stances.
     * 165 External Benchmark Test Images: 20 Handgun, 85 Knife, 60 Held Non-Weapon Negatives.

7. Historical Prototype Datasets (Models 1 to 4 Archive):
   - Open Images V6 Weapon Subset: Google Open Images via FiftyOne (1,591 images stored locally).
   - ARMAS Dataset: Roboflow public firearm subset (548 images).
   - Hugging Face Weapon Dataset: Web-scraped weapon dataset (598 images); revealed the 207
     corrupted coordinates that caused the Model 3 Handgun collapse.

SECTION 6: ARCHITECTURAL & TRAINING SPECIFICATIONS (MODEL 10)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Architecture:           Faster R-CNN with ResNet-50 Feature Pyramid Network (FPN v2)
- Anchor Scale Pyramid:   ((16,), (32,), (64,), (128,), (256,))
- Anchor Aspect Ratios:   ((0.5, 1.0, 2.0),) * 5
- RPN BBox Transform Clip:ln(2.2) ≈ 0.7885 (permits 256px anchor to reach 563px blades)
- RoI BBox Transform Clip:ln(2.2) ≈ 0.7885 (prevents box dilation on noise)
- Optimizer:              SGD (Base lr=0.00012, momentum=0.9, weight_decay=0.0005)
- Learning Rate Schedule: CosineAnnealingLR (1.2e-4 -> 1e-6 over 4 epochs)
- Batch Size:             2 (accum_steps=1)
- Workstation GPU:        NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Ti (16 GB GDDR6 VRAM, CUDA 13.0)
- CPU Thread Cap:         Strictly capped at 8 threads (OMP_NUM_THREADS=8)
- Data Augmentations:     1D Linear PSF Motion Blur (5-9px, 0-180 deg),
                          Synthetic Blade Edge Glint (1.2x - 1.45x boost),
                          Specular Highlight & Metal Tone Jitter (±25%),
                          Synthetic Hand-Grip Occlusion (CutMix)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 7: KEY EMPIRICAL FINDINGS & DEFENSE TAKEAWAYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Finding 1: The Multi-Scale Anchor Receptive Field Trade-Off
- Models 1-6 lacked regression limits, causing bounding boxes to explode across rooms.
- Model 7 added unconstrained 8px anchors, triggering 134 false alarms on screws and bolts.
- Model 9 clamped to ln(1.4) and cut anchors to 128px, achieving 94.09% precision but
  truncating large knives (59 knife false negatives).
- Model 10 restored 256px anchors with calibrated ln(2.2) clamping, achieving 95.22% mAP
  on the M9 benchmark and 97.71% mAP on unseen CCTV, solving both box explosions and knife dropout.

Finding 2: Confounder-Specific Negative Mining
- Traditional background negative mining uses empty rooms. The Held Non-Weapon Grid Audit
  proved that everyday objects held in human hands (phones, bottles, wallets) pose the
  highest risk of false alarms.
- Injecting 450 held non-weapon confounders raised grid specificity to 94.44% and real
  CCTV specificity to 100% (zero false alarms across 1,517 frames).

Finding 3: Scientific Validation of Class Difficulty Gap
- Across all 10 models and literature (Fernandez-Carrobles et al.), handguns consistently
  achieve higher AP than knives in unconstrained CCTV.
- Handguns maintain a rigid, volumetric silhouette with high feature density, whereas
  knives have thin cross-sections, metallic specular reflections, and hand occlusion.
- This scientifically validates the necessity of BasicVSR++ super-resolution and
  Temporal Consistency Filtering in FORENSIKADA.
